Why STRATOS?

Technology recommendations built around your business, not a sales quota.

STRATOS Consulting Group, LLC is a Puerto Rico-based information technology consulting firm. We work closely with each organization to understand its environment, priorities, risks, and budget before recommending a solution.

In some cases, the right answer is a new platform or a full modernization project. In others, the best decision is to strengthen what's already working. Our role is to help you make informed technology decisions, with clarity and confidence.

We don't just sell technology. We build long-term, trusted relationships through honest guidance, practical execution, and responsible support.
